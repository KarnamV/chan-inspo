Demon Realm Escape: plan

Isometric 3D action roguelite, Godot 4.7. Jackie, a stunt double, fights through demon realms with whatever is lying around. This page holds every plan, its status and progress. Last updated .

Now

State: Milestones M0–M4 are built and verified. Realm 1 plays end to end: tea house → 10 rooms → Butcher King → back to the tea house. After your first playtest (2026-10-10) the combat was reworked (talismans on cooldowns, input buffer, dodge cancels, softer demons), then gamepad support, an art and UI polish pass, and an illustrated story intro were added.

Checks: 37/37 unit tests; all four gameplay bots pass (3/3 runs). A real-HP bot that never dodges reaches room 9 of 10.

Waiting on you: replay with a gamepad and judge feel, the new look and the intro. See Waiting on you.

How we work

  • This page is the plan. Update it whenever progress changes: tick tasks, add new ones, rewrite Now. Decisions and lessons go to design/CHANGELOG.md.
  • Per milestone: generate that milestone's assets, review renders and re-roll bad ones, build, then run the checks and a playable gate.
  • Checks (in game/): import, unit tests, the four gameplay bots (3 runs after balance changes), and the fair probe bot_run -- --fair before any hand-off. Commands are in design/tech.md.
  • Show, don't tell: visual changes are checked from captured frames or video, never assumed.
  • ComfyUI runs only while generating and is stopped once the last generation is done.
  • Git: commit locally when asked; never push without asking.
